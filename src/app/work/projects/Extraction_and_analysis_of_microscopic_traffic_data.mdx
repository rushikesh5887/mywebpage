## Project Summary

I built `VeTre`, a semi-automated pipeline for extracting microscopic vehicle trajectories from mixed-traffic video, where dense interactions and occlusion made fully automated tracking unreliable. The workflow combined analyst-guided tracking, camera calibration, and image-to-road projection to convert raw footage into structured trajectory data.

I then used `MATLAB` and `R` to turn those trajectories into interaction datasets, engineered features, and study-ready outputs for following, staggered-following, and lateral-movement analysis. This is one of the clearest examples in the portfolio of building a full raw-to-structured data workflow for difficult real-world conditions.

## Business and Research Impact

This work made high-resolution traffic data collection practical in lane-less and heterogeneous traffic conditions that standard tools struggle to capture. For a `30`-minute video, the pipeline reduced processing time to about `20` skilled hours compared with roughly `100` hours reported for similar semi-automatic approaches.

The resulting data supported behavior modeling, traffic simulation calibration, and safety analysis. Archived outputs from the codebase include `700` trajectory files and more than `300k` frame-level records in one processed batch, showing the value of a reusable analytical pipeline instead of a one-off extraction workflow.

## What I built

- **Trajectory extraction pipeline:** Built a `Python` workflow that converted raw traffic video into structured frame-level trajectory data for downstream analysis.
- **Large-scale dataset generation:** Produced and validated a `5M+` datapoint dataset to support behavior analysis, modeling, and calibration work.
- **Feature engineering layer:** Used `MATLAB` to derive interaction features for following behavior, lateral positioning, lateral shift propensity, density, and speed across vehicle classes.
- **Measurement validation:** Applied camera calibration and coordinate mapping to recover roadway positions from image space and improve data reliability.
- **Repeatable processing workflow:** Designed the system to generate hundreds of trajectory files, merge intermediate datasets, segment interactions by vehicle class, and export analysis-ready outputs.
- **Workflow automation:** Reduced a representative extraction workflow from about `27` manual clicks to `3` clicks for a short tracked segment while still supporting multi-object tracking.
- **Reporting outputs:** Used `MATLAB` and `R` to generate summary statistics, analytical outputs, and publication-ready visualizations.
